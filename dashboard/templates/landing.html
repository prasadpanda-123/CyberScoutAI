What is CyberScout AI?

Finding high-quality cybersecurity opportunities, internships, CTF competitions, and free learning resources usually requires checking dozens of separate forums, GitHub repositories, RSS feeds, and social channels every day.

CyberScout AI automates this tedious discovery process. By combining multi-source harvesting with AI-assisted deduplication and scoring engines, CyberScout AI aggregates security opportunities into a structured intelligence repository—saving researchers, students, and practitioners hundreds of hours.

Continuous Web Harvesting

Monitors RSS vulnerability feeds, security blogs, GitHub repos, CTF portals, and academic challenge boards 24/7.

Quality & Spam Filtering

Automatically evaluates confidence metrics, eliminating expired listings, duplicate links, and promotional noise.

Structured Categorization

Organizes items into clear domains: Internships, CTFs, Certifications, Courses, Scholarships, and Webinars.
