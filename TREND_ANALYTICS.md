# Trend Analytics & Skill Growth Specification (`TREND_ANALYTICS.md`)

## Overview

The **Trend Detector** (`trend_detector.py`) aggregates opportunity tags, company names, categories, and provider metrics to provide insight into cybersecurity skill demand and hiring trends.

---

## Analytics Categories

1. **Top Growing Skills**: Most frequent technology & skill tags (`python`, `pentest`, `dfir`, `cloud-security`, `reverse-engineering`).
2. **Top Hiring Companies**: Organizations listing the highest number of cybersecurity opportunities.
3. **Top Categories**: Distribution across CTFs, internships, certifications, bug bounties, and research papers.
4. **Provider Velocity**: Growth velocity of individual collection sources.
